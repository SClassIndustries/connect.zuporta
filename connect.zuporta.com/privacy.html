Privacy Policy

Zuporta+ · Last updated [DATE OF APPROVAL]

Zuporta+ is a personal wellness app made by Lyfemind Coaching Collective LLC ("Zuporta", "we"). This policy explains what information the Zuporta+ app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. It covers the Zuporta+ app on the web, iPhone, iPad and Android. Coaching sessions you book on zuporta.com are covered by the zuporta.com privacy policy.

The short version

  • What you type into Zuporta+ — check-ins, mood and sleep ratings, answers to guided activities and your conversation with the assistant — is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.
  • The assistant runs on your device. Your messages are not sent to us or to any AI service.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your wellness history at any time from Settings → My Data.

  • Links you open. Signing in, creating an account and booking a coach happen on zuporta.com in your browser. Crisis-line links open findahelpline.com. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your privacy choices

Optional Personalize my support uses guided activities you rated helpful to suggest an activity on Home. It is off until you choose to enable it, works only on your device, and can be turned off in Settings → Manage permissions. With it off, Home suggests a standard activity. You can continue using the app either way.

The app does not offer research, model-training or professional-sharing permissions because it does not send your wellness entries for those purposes. Choices recorded by older versions for these unavailable features are discarded when the app loads; your wellness records are retained.

In the iPhone and Android app, daily reminders are off by default. Enabling a reminder in Settings requests notification permission. You choose the local time and can turn it off there; device settings may delay or block delivery. Notifications contain generic text, never your name, answers or wellness history. Haptic feedback during breathing can also be turned off in Settings. Reminders and haptics do not send data to us.

Share exercise opens your device's share sheet (or copies the instructions in a browser without sharing support). Only public exercise instructions are included, never your entries. If you choose a recipient or service, it receives the instructions under its own privacy policy.

Health information

Wellness check-ins can reveal things about your health. Zuporta+ is a wellness tool, not a medical service, and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Because your entries stay on your device, we do not hold your health information.

Deleting your information

  • In the app: Settings → My Data → Delete My Wellness History removes check-ins, conversations, guided activity records, module and baseline history, follow-ups, the local activity log and privacy choices. It keeps your chosen name, display theme, reminder/haptics preferences and cached public coach directory. Turn off a scheduled reminder separately in Settings. To remove all site data, use your browser’s site-data controls.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing site data in your browser, also removes it.
  • If you have a zuporta.com account, the published account data access and deletion instructions direct requests to privacy@zuporta.com. Deleting local wellness history does not delete your website account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Because app data stays on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app. For anything we hold, such as a zuporta.com account, contact privacy@zuporta.com, as listed in the website privacy policy. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
