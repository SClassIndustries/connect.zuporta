# Unreleased source archive

These service-flow modules, fictional fixtures and unfinished app panels
are preserved for future development. They are outside `connect.zuporta.com`
and are not served or copied into either native app. They are reference
source, not standalone runnable features. Their imports may need restoration
when real content/integrations are built.

Do not re-enable them just by restoring a route. Follow the release-scope
notice in `STORE_READINESS.md`, complete the missing service integrations,
remove all fictional data, and validate the resulting product first.
Existing private module records on a user's device are preserved by the
release app until that user deletes wellness history.
