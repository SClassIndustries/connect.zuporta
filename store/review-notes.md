# Store reviewer notes

## App access

Zuporta+ works without creating an account. Home, guided activities, local
mood history, Personal Baseline and Settings can be exercised without login.
The guide is deterministic and runs on the device. No AI API is called.

For a guided interaction, open Home → Start, select a before rating and
context, complete the activity, then choose your own after rating and
helpfulness response. My Wellness and Sessions display those local records.
Personal Baseline needs at least three check-ins before a comparison is
meaningful. Do not pre-populate fictional health results for review.

Settings → Daily check-in reminder enables an optional generic device
notification at the user's chosen local time. Notification permission is
requested only after enabling. Exact alarms are not requested. Breathing
haptics can be disabled, and Share exercise sends only public instructions.

Settings links the privacy policy, terms, support and website-account
deletion instructions. Delete My Wellness History removes local wellness
records; reminder, haptics and diagnostics preferences remain separately
controlled as described in the app policy.

## Human coaching and payments

The public directory is fetched from api.zuporta.com. Coach buttons open
the selected profile on zuporta.com using the platform browser. The website
may require sign-in; after signing in, reopen the profile link if necessary.
Coach availability/prices are confirmed there. No local wellness record is
sent to the website, and a native/website login session is not silently shared.

This companion offers no digital goods, paid app feature, subscription or
purchase/restore button. Linked coaching services have their own pricing.
If these are live services between two individuals, explain that scope under
Apple 3.1.3(d) and the applicable Google policy. The owner must confirm the
actual linked services are within that exception; recorded content,
one-to-many sessions or digital memberships require a new billing review.

If reviewers need a website account to examine the linked booking service,
provide working limited review access securely in the store console. Do
not add production/admin passwords to this repository or public notes.

## Wellness scope and safety

This is a general wellness tool, not therapy, a medical device, diagnostic
software or emergency response. No person monitors the guide. Explicit
crisis phrases interrupt the exercise path with immediate human-support
resources; the rule-based matcher cannot detect every emergency. Device QA
and appropriate review of content/resources remain required.

## Optional diagnostics

Configured native builds offer Sentry crash/hang reports and session counts,
off until the user enables them. No replay, screenshots, app-entry upload,
default user identity, click breadcrumbs or tracing is enabled. JavaScript
errors are reduced to generic error/code locations. Native technical crash
reports remain native SDK reports and must be covered by the final labels.

## Before copying these notes into a console

- Insert the exact version/build number and tested device coverage.
- Verify production policy/support URLs and all external account links.
- Confirm US availability and finish age/content/health/privacy forms.
- State the actual Sentry configuration and avoid claiming monitored
  stability percentages until a dated sample with a denominator exists.
- Include reviewer contact name and international-format phone number.
