## Full description for both stores

Take a moment to check in with yourself.

Zuporta+ brings together short guided wellness activities, mood check-ins
and Personal Baseline reflections. Use it to put your thoughts in order,
try a breathing exercise, or choose a small next step.

- Record how you feel and review check-ins saved on this device.
- Try structured guided resets and rate how helpful they felt to you.
- Build a Personal Baseline from your own check-ins.
- Use the core wellness tools offline after installation.
- Set an optional daily reminder in the iPhone or Android app.
- Use optional breathing haptics and share public exercise instructions.
- Browse Zuporta coaches and open the selected coach on zuporta.com.

Your wellness entries and guided conversation stay on your device. The
guide uses rules on your device; it does not send your conversation to an
AI model. Optional crash diagnostics, when configured and enabled, send
technical stability reports to Sentry. You control this in Settings.

Coaching profiles, sign-in, availability, prices, booking and payment are
handled on zuporta.com in a separate browser. A website account and
internet connection may be needed for those services. This app does not
share your local wellness entries with a coach.

Zuporta+ provides general wellness support. It is not medical care,
therapy, diagnosis or emergency assistance. It cannot reliably detect
every crisis, and no person monitors your conversation. Seek qualified
care when you need it.
